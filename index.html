Strength
& Reactivation

June 2 → June 29

Foundation is in. Now we get heavier and we put the spring back in the system. Strength shifts to 3–5 rep ranges. Plyometrics return at low volume — landing mechanics first, height second. One basketball practice/week becomes two. Hip hinges go heavier than knee-dominant work because the quad tendon still needs respect. By the end of this phase, the squat 1RM should be approaching 130 kg.

// Plyo Reintroduction Protocol
Plyometrics return ONLY if Week 4 checkpoint is hit. Start with extensive (low intensity, high quality) before intensive. Pogos and low boxes weeks 5–6. Approach jumps and depth drops weeks 7–8. Volume cap: 80 ground contacts/session, never two plyo days back-to-back.

Power
& Conditioning

June 30 → July 27

Strength is in the bank. Now we turn it into basketball power. Olympic lift derivatives, weighted jumps, depth jumps, sled work. Court conditioning becomes serious — repeat-sprint ability, defensive slides under fatigue, full-court transition reps. Lifting volume drops slightly, intensity stays high. Two basketball practices per week become standard. By the end of Phase 3, the body should look and move like a player again.

// The Real Battle
Conditioning is the gap between you and your peers. The men's team is too slow to test it; the U18 sessions matter more here. Aim to outwork the 18-year-olds. If you can't yet, that's the data — keep pushing repeat-sprint capacity. Vertical jump should jump in this phase as reactive strength reconnects with raw force.

// When to Adjust the Program
  • → Joint pain > 3/10 for 2+ sessions: drop that movement, sub a regression, retest in a week.
  • → Missing a strength target by 5+ kg: hold the load, repeat the week. Don't push.
  • → Sleep < 7h for 3+ nights: drop the next plyo session, replace with skill work.
  • → Bodyweight up > 1 kg week-on-week: recheck calories. Likely overshooting weekend.
  • → Bodyweight down > 1.5 kg week-on-week: too aggressive — add 200 kcal/day for 5 days.
  • → Glute medius flare-up: immediate 5-day deload, glute rehab 2x daily, return to W2 progression.
  • → Two practices in a row feel "flat": probably CNS fatigue. Take 48h with no plyo or sprint.
// What NOT to do
  • → Don't change the program because of one bad week. Trends over 2-3 weeks only.
  • → Don't add extra sessions because you "feel good" in Phase 4. The temptation will be enormous. Resist it.
  • → Don't return to 18:6 fasting until well into the season at maintenance.
  • → Don't test 1RM more often than the schedule says — every test costs 5-7 days of recovery.
  • → Don't compare yourself to the 23-year-old version of you. Compare to last week.
  • → Don't skip glute medius work even when it feels stupid. It's prevention, not performance.
  • → Don't squat heavy if Copenhagen plank time-under-tension is dropping. That's the canary.
